Privacy Policy for Family Expense Tracker

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Package: com.oneeye.familytracker

Summary: Family Expense Tracker is an offline-first household expense tracker. The developer does not operate servers, collect analytics, serve advertisements, or receive any of your financial data.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

All expense entries, categories, family member names, monthly budgets, and settings are stored locally inside an SQLite database on your device.

2. Camera & Photo Library (Optional Receipt Scanning)

If you choose to scan a receipt or bill:

  • Camera or gallery access is requested only when you tap the scan option.
  • Receipt text recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition.
  • Receipt images are never attached to saved expenses and are not uploaded to the developer.

3. Files & Optional Google Drive Sync

If you choose to back up data or use Google Drive sync:

  • You explicitly choose the destination file using Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework).
  • The app only accesses the specific file you select (family-expenses-sync.json or exported JSON/CSV backups).
  • Synced files remain in your own Google Drive account or device storage under your control.

4. SMS, Banking & Background Tracking

Family Expense Tracker does not request SMS permissions, does not read text messages, and does not connect to any bank accounts.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with third parties. The app contains no advertising SDKs.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual expenses anytime inside the app, unlink your sync file in Google Drive sync settings, or delete all local app data by uninstalling the app.
